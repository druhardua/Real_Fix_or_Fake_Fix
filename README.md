# Real Fix or Fake Fix?

![Cartoon computer gadgets with friendly faces sit on a desk under the game's title: a worried blue-screen monitor, a smiling laptop, a printer, a coffee mug, and a mouse next to a wedge of cheese.](cover.svg)

Real Fix or Fake Fix? is a 10-minute help-desk party game for groups of coworkers, where you spot the real tech-support fixes and the silly fake ones.

## Play it

**[Add your game's web address here]**

You need one phone or computer for the host (sharing the screen on a video call works well) and 2 to 10 players.

## How to play

1. The host opens the game, picks the number of players, and taps **Start**. Each person is Player 1, Player 2, and so on.
2. Each ticket shows a tech problem and four fixes, A to D. A banner says whether to find the one **fake** fix or the one **real** fix.
3. Everyone calls out a letter. The host taps each player's letter in the **Who said what?** box.
4. The host taps **Reveal answer**. Everyone who picked the right letter gets 1 point.
5. Tap **Next ticket**. After 8 tickets, the player with the most points wins.
